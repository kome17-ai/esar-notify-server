Origin

A house first.
An app second.

ESAR started from a simple fatigue: five icons for one life. A feed in one place, a car in another, a chat in a third, videos in a fourth, a stall in a fifth. Each company asked for a new password and a new version of you.

The house idea is older than software. You walk into a building. Rooms have different jobs. You do not change your name at every doorway.

What the rooms borrowed

Social borrowed the calm of a familiar feed — friends, groups, photos — without turning the hallway into a billboard.

Rides borrowed the city reflex: tap a destination, watch a car approach, share the trip so someone at home can see you arrive.

Chat borrowed the green bubble: fast messages, family groups, voice notes that feel like a kitchen conversation.

Play borrowed the red triangle: channels, playlists, and videos a family can actually watch together.

Market borrowed the stall: list something you own, find something nearby, talk to the seller in the same chat world.

Rules of the house

One account. One wallet. Kind comments. Verified drivers. No dark rooms. If a world is not safe for a younger person, it does not ship.

The application is how you carry the keys. This website is how you understand the building.
