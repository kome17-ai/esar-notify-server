Questions

Usually asked.

Is this only an app page?

No. The site is the house: story, worlds, journal, and desk. The stores are one shelf in the hallway.

What worlds exist today?

Social (feed), Rides (city cars), Chat (messages), Play (video), Market (buy and sell), and Pay (wallet).

Is it alright for younger people?

The house is designed family-first: safer defaults, trip sharing, and moderated video. Parents still decide who gets an account.

Can I change the ads banner?

Yes. Edit the ads list in js/main.js. The top strip is meant for house notes and partner lines.
